Arch isn't for everyone, and that's fine. Here's why it works for me.

## The pitch

Arch gives you a minimal base and lets you build up from there. Nothing is
installed that you didn't explicitly ask for. That sounds tedious until you
realize it means you actually **understand your own system**.

## The trade-off

- You will read the wiki. A lot.
- You will occasionally break your system on a Tuesday.
- You will learn more in a month than in a year on a "just works" distro.

## Who it's for

If you like knowing what's running on your machine and why, Arch rewards
that curiosity. If you just want a laptop that boots and gets out of your
way, look elsewhere — and that's a completely valid choice too.

The best distro is the one that matches how you actually want to spend your
time. For me, right now, that's Arch.
